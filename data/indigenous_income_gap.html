About this data

Data are from Statistics Canada's Canadian Income Survey (CIS), table 11-10-0091-01, comparing the Indigenous population (First Nations, Métis and Inuit combined) to the non-Indigenous population, Canada, 2012–2024. Figures are in 2024 constant dollars.

The CIS excludes persons living on reserves and other Indigenous settlements in the provinces. Because a substantial share of First Nations people live on reserve, this series understates the income gap facing that population specifically; it is best read as a lower bound on the overall Indigenous/non-Indigenous gap.
